Chitlapakkam Lake restoration

Since 2019 I have volunteered with Chitlapakkam Rising, a residents' group in south Chennai. The local lake had become a dumping ground, with garbage on the bed and sewage flowing in.

We cleaned the lake bed week in and week out. The group started with about ten people and grew to around six hundred, and that effort helped the state government sanction Rs. 25 crore to desilt and restore the lake. Today it is a public space people use every day, and its clean water helps recharge local groundwater. I was one of many people who made that happen.
